Logis privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Logis is a single-user personal app. Its only user is its owner, and it is not offered to anyone else.

What Logis accesses

Logis reads only its owner's Gmail (and Google Calendar, if access is granted), on the owner's own computer, to prepare a daily briefing.

How data is used and shared

No data is sold, shared or sent to third parties, except Google's own APIs and the AI model providers the owner has configured to summarise mail.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
